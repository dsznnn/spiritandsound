About Spirit & Sound

Spirit & Sound is the independent creative group behind a collection of ventures connected by music, atmosphere and culture. Each brand has its own identity, purpose and audience, while sharing a wider creative direction.

The group brings together Solace, Zen And Friends and ZORA. Rather than making every venture look or feel the same, Spirit & Sound gives each one room to develop on its own terms while remaining part of a broader vision.

Our point of view

We believe the details matter: the sound, the setting, the people and the feeling a moment leaves behind. Our approach is considered and independent, with music and atmosphere at the centre of what we build.

How we work
