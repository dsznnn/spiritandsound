About

Spirit & Sound is an independent platform focused on Afro House, Afro Tech and electronic music culture.

We document artists, events, venues, collectives and communities shaping the sound of today and tomorrow.

Mission

To support and contribute to the growth of Afro electronic music culture by creating a space where stories, people and music can be discovered and shared.

Founded by Johannesburg-based Afro Tech and Afro House DJ SEN-X, Spirit & Sound reflects a passion for immersive electronic music. Built around authenticity, atmosphere, and connection, it showcases carefully curated sounds, meaningful experiences, and the evolving journey of an artist dedicated to pushing South Africa's electronic music culture forward.
