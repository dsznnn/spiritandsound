Want to be involved? Here's how:

Events

If you’re interested in performing at an S&S event, please reach out to us via Instagram DM at @spiritandsoundjhb. Include your rates and some of your previous work when you message us.

Interviews

If you’re looking to be featured in an interview with S&S, please contact us via Instagram DM at @spiritandsoundjhb. Make sure to include a short introduction about yourself and any relevant links to your previous work or projects.

Private Sets

If you’re interested in booking a private set with S&S, please reach out to us via Instagram DM at @spiritandsoundjhb. Include your preferred dates, set duration, location, and your rates when you message us.

Collaborations

If you’re interested in collaborating with S&S, please contact us via Instagram DM at @spiritandsoundjhb. Include a short introduction about yourself or your project, along with any relevant links, ideas, or previous work when you reach out.
