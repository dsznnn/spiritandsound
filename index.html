About How We Do Things
Written By ZEN

Spirit & Sound began in 2025 with its first private set. The event was well received and helped shape the direction of the platform.

Built around atmospheric, percussion-driven music, Spirit & Sound creates experiences where people can properly connect with the music. The platform focuses on the energy, rhythm, groove and deep basslines that define many of South Africa's most exciting sounds.

Spirit & Sound exists to highlight passionate DJs, distinctive sounds and the culture behind them, while showcasing the depth and diversity of South Africa's music scene.
