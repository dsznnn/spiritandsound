A little about us

Spirit & Sound began in 2025 with its first private set, laying the foundation for an independent platform built around music, atmosphere and intentional experiences.

Rooted in atmospheric, percussion-driven sounds, Spirit & Sound explores the relationship between music, energy and connection. Every experience is shaped by rhythm, groove, deep basslines and the details that make a moment feel distinct.

At its core, Spirit & Sound is about celebrating the depth of South Africa’s electronic music culture, highlighting passionate DJs and distinctive sounds while creating experiences that leave a lasting impression.
