About ZORAH

ZORAH is an independent electronic music label built around artists with a distinct voice, a strong sense of rhythm and music that exists beyond simple genre definitions.

Based in Johannesburg, the label operates at the intersection of African electronic music and the wider global underground.

We release music we believe deserves to be heard.
